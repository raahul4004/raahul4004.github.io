## Raahul Muthukrishnan

Machine learning research — computer vision, multimodal models, and why models stop working on data they haven't seen.

Currently an applied science intern at Amazon working on detection of AI-generated images. The problem that keeps me there: a detector can report near-perfect accuracy in a paper and then fall apart on images from a generator it wasn't trained on. Most benchmarks never test for that, so the failure stays invisible until it matters.

Master's student in AI & ML at VIT. First author on a manuscript in preparation.
